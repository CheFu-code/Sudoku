## What stays on your device

Sudoku has bundled puzzles and does not require an account or our SSO.
The board, pencil notes, elapsed time, mistakes, hints used, undo/redo history,
played-puzzle identifiers, completion counts, best times, achievements and
unlocked difficulties are handled locally.

Native saved games, progress, and settings use a SQLite-backed store. Web builds
use browser local storage where available. There is no account-based cloud
backup or cross-device sync from us.

## What can leave your device

The app starts **Sentry diagnostics and mobile session replay automatically**
unless disabled in Settings (changes apply after the next app launch).
Its current configuration enables default personal-information context and logs,
samples ordinary replays at 10%, and enables error-associated replay sampling at
100%, subject to SDK/platform limitations. Reports can contain errors, app and
device details, identifiers, IP/network context and diagnostic breadcrumbs or log
entries where generated. Replays can show aspects of the app interface and
interactions; SDK masking is not a guarantee that every detail is hidden.
A feedback integration is installed; information submitted through a feedback
form, if presented, can also be sent to Sentry.

The app provides diagnostics and replay switches in Settings, but no age check
or verified parental-consent flow. Reading a notice or accepting terms does not replace
consent where applicable law requires it.

Native builds check for compatible **Expo EAS Updates** on launch. Requests can
include IP address, a generated installation identifier, platform, runtime version
and update identifiers. Update code and assets can be cached locally.

Settings links open websites with their own privacy practices. Tapping the
version information writes app version/build and OS details to the clipboard;
it does not read existing clipboard contents or submit a support report.
If you contact us, we receive the message, address and attachments you send.

There are no advertising networks, targeted ads or in-app payment features in
the current game. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local games and statistics remain until replaced or removed. Clear app storage
on Android or site storage in the browser to remove local records. Starting a new
game does not erase all statistics. Device backup/restore settings can preserve
or restore copies; the Android app permits OS backup. We cannot recover
device-only records.

Diagnostics, replay and correspondence are kept as needed for app operation,
fault investigation and the request concerned. We delete or de-identify personal
information when no longer needed or when a verified deletion request is handled,
except relevant records required for a specific legal obligation or claim.
No fixed Sentry retention period is represented here.

Uninstalling does not automatically erase reports or correspondence already
received online. Contact **privacy@chefu.co.za** for information requests and
identify Sudoku, the approximate date and any report reference available.

## Children and contact

Children may play Sudoku, with parent or guardian authorisation where required.
Parents should review the automatic online processing before use. A statement
requiring permission is not a verified parental-consent process.
